BYTEZONE POLICY

Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and keep your account information protected. This page explains how ByteZone handles the information you provide while using the store.

01

Information We Collect

We use the information you provide, such as your name, email and delivery details, to create your account, process orders and provide support.

02

How We Use It

Your information is used to authenticate your account, fulfill purchases, communicate about orders and improve the ByteZone experience.

03

Payments & Security

Authentication and account requests are handled through secured application endpoints. Never share your password with anyone, including support.

04

Your Choices

You can update your profile information from your account page. For privacy questions or requests, contact the ByteZone support team.
